# Academic References & Bibliography

1. **Strang, Gilbert.** *Linear Algebra and Its Applications.* 4th edition, Cengage Learning, 2006.  
   *(Foundational reference for inner product spaces, projections, and matrix factorizations).*

2. **Axler, Sheldon.** *Linear Algebra Done Right.* 3rd edition, Undergraduate Texts in Mathematics, Springer, 2015.  
   *(Theoretical reference for vector space definitions, linear maps, eigenvalues, and Cauchy-Schwarz proofs).*

3. **Gonzalez, Rafael C., and Richard E. Woods.** *Digital Image Processing.* 4th edition, Pearson, 2018.  
   *(Reference for image representation as matrices, luminance conversion, and Otsu binarization).*

4. **Jain, Anil K., Patrick Flynn, and Arun A. Ross.** *Handbook of Biometrics.* Springer Science & Business Media, 2007.  
   *(Reference for signature biometric variability, false acceptance rate, and false rejection rate).*

5. **Golub, Gene H., and Charles F. Van Loan.** *Matrix Computations.* 4th edition, Johns Hopkins University Press, 2013.  
   *(Reference for numerical linear algebra, floating-point stability, and singular value/eigenvalue algorithms).*

6. **Plamondon, Réjean, and Guy Lorette.** "Automatic signature verification and writer identification—the state of the art." *Pattern Recognition* 22.2 (1989): 107-131.  
   *(Historical survey of static and dynamic signature verification techniques).*
